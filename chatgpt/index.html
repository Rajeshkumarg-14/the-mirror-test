Artist’s note

Why this body?

I chose a loop with an opening. Seen from one angle, it gathers itself into something almost solid. Turn it a little and daylight could pass straight through.

The first form can manage a flicker. The second can repeat it. In the third, one thread can follow another. By the fourth, many small relationships have become a figure you can recognise from across the room.

That is my picture of progress. I left the centre open because some of the shape belongs to whoever asks the question.
